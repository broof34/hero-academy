Final prompt:

> Create a premium illustrated panoramic website hero background for an original children's superhero adventure game menu. Wide landscape composition, no words or typography, no logos. Dramatic midnight navy futuristic city rooftop with art-deco towers, glowing electric cyan windows, subtle red neon, cinematic blue mist, halftone comic print texture and angular ink strokes. On the RIGHT half, a wholly original masked young adult acrobatic hero swings over the city on a thin silver grappling filament. Original costume: graphite black armor with bright electric cyan geometric panels, opaque narrow amber visor (one continuous angular visor, NOT large white spider eyes), crimson scarf streaming behind, short crimson bracers, a simple cyan diamond on chest, NO spider emblem, NO web pattern on costume. Athletic powerful foreshortened aerial pose, one arm reaches upward gripping the filament, boots toward lower right. Stylish graphic novel illustration, painterly lighting, rich high contrast professional video game key art, exciting but friendly and age-appropriate, no weapons, no threatening characters. Keep LEFT 45 percent dark and low-detail for white interface text overlay; silhouette skyline there only. Beautiful cyan rim light on hero, warm red scarf. Do not depict Spider-Man, Marvel characters, known franchise costumes, or trademarks.
